# Bulutla home: brief

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The owner asked to convert the
remaining pages "the way Codex did", with big animations on by default, built
with scroll-craft to the standard of the approved collection reel
(Serein, Glaido, PERKFORM, Sonder, FORME, OFFGRID). No interview was run.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Quiet archive room at night. Paper, folders, a stamp. References:
   a film archive's index cards, a museum drawer, an architect's plan chest.
   (Authored; continues the "proje arşivi" world Codex established.)
2. **Journey.** See the folder open, recognise your own file chaos, watch it get
   filed, choose a shelf size, see your own numbers, open your folder.
3. **Energy.** Calm open, tense second beat, loudest at the filing, calm range,
   quiet close.
4. **Feeling + one moment.** See curve below. Moment: the filing.
5. **What no other site does.** Your scroll files the mess. Every scattered file
   flies into one folder and the tab counts them in.
6. **Range.** Editorial premium, dark, restrained. Not neon, not AI-purple glow.
7. **One world or scenes?** Distinct scenes inside one room (acts, drift in one
   dark family).
8. **Assets.** None photographic. No KIE key. The world is the illustrated paper
   archive already in the codebase, rebuilt as independent CSS planes.

## Grammar

Filmic one-shot. One linear argument (scattered files, then one archive) with one
arc. Chaptered editorial lost: there is no long-form substance to read. Live
surface lost: the real product sits behind login and a painted demo is banned.
Continuous world lost: no geography. Typographic poster lost: the folder object
is the brand's asset. Gallery lost: the page argues, it does not just list.
Split stage lost: there is a before/after, but the filing peak needs one
centred stage. Cutlist lost: wrong energy for an archive.

## Feeling curve

| Act | Feeling | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Hero | curiosity | the folder opens under the hand, sheets rise across the headline (pin + parallax planes + pointer depth) |
| 2 Tension | recognition, slight unease | a desk of misnamed files wipes in while three short lines name the mess (flow + reveal) |
| 3 Peak | relief, satisfaction | scroll files every loose file into one folder, the tab counts them, the stamp lands (pin, signature move, kinetic) |
| 4 Range | confidence | plans travel past laterally like shelves in a plan chest (pan + tilt) |
| 5 Account (signed in) | ownership | the visitor's real storage and project numbers tick in (flow + count) |
| 6 Close | calm resolve | one closed folder, one action, light follows the pointer (flow + spotlight + magnet) |

**Peak.** "Sayfayı kaydırdıkça masadaki bütün dağınık dosyalar tek tek klasöre
uçup dosyalanıyor, sonra damga basılıyor." Lives in act 3, largest span (3.4).

**Tell-someone sentence.** It's the site where your scroll tidies up your files.

**Authored silence.** None.
